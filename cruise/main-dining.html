Main dining dinner menu samples

Norwegian Getaway’s three complimentary main dining rooms offer table service and share a dinner menu:

  • Taste — Deck 6.
  • Savor — Deck 6.
  • The Manhattan Room — Deck 7.

The dinner menu changes nightly. This page collects two samples for all three restaurants. Check the onboard daily schedule for restaurant opening times.

These are examples from undated menu photographs shared publicly for Norwegian Getaway. The archive lists a July 5, 2026 upload; the sources were checked on August 30, 2026. They do not confirm the menu, prices, or sequence for a future sailing. This page covers dinner.

Dietary symbols appear as text, and asterisks link to the food safety notice. Descriptions retain the listed ingredients in plain language. Optional extra-charge dishes and wines are separated below.

Dietary labels, charges, and food safety notices

Dietary symbols are written out as “Gluten free,” “Contains alcohol,” “Contains nuts,” “Spicy,” and “Vegetarian.” Only symbols printed beside the dishes are reproduced. An omitted label does not establish that a dish is free from an allergen or alcohol, or suitable for a particular diet. The no-sugar-added desserts are identified separately.

Tell your server about food allergies before ordering. Confirm ingredients and preparation with the restaurant.

The photographed menus print a 20% gratuity and beverage service charge, possible additional tax in certain ports or itineraries, and a limit of one entrée per guest with a $5 charge for additional entrées. The photographs do not clearly explain which selections the entrée charge applies to. These sample notices are not a verified statement of current main dining room policy; ask staff about any charge before ordering.

Raw or undercooked food advisory: Items linked to this notice are marked with an asterisk in the source. They may be served raw or undercooked, or contain ingredients prepared that way. Raw or undercooked meat, seafood, shellfish, eggs, milk, and poultry can carry a risk of foodborne illness, especially for people with certain medical conditions.
