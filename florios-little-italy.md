# Florio's of Little Italy Menu

Screen-reader-friendly version for Florio's of Little Italy on the Hollywood Beach Broadwalk.

Last checked: August 21, 2026. The restaurant's printed menu carries the date March 2026.

## Location And Phone

- 200 Garfield Street, Hollywood, Florida 33019
- 954-925-0004 is the number on the restaurant's own menu and website.
- 954-507-9413 is the number listed on the Slice ordering page.

## Hours

The restaurant's website says Florio's is open every day and that the menu is served from 12 noon until close.

These are the hours the Slice ordering page lists for delivery and for pickup. Both services show the same times.

- Monday through Thursday, 12:00 noon to 9:00 PM
- Friday and Saturday, 12:00 noon to 10:00 PM
- Sunday, 12:00 noon to 9:00 PM

Call the restaurant to confirm dine-in hours.

## Good To Know

- The restaurant serves dine-in, takeout, and delivery.
- Gratuity is added to each check.
- The Lunch Only section is served daily from 12 noon to 4:00 PM.
- Gluten free pizza crust and vegan cheese are available on request.
- Salads take added chicken for $6.00 or added shrimp for $7.00.
- Pizza comes in two sizes: large at 14 inches and extra large at 18 inches.
- The menu invites you to ask if you don't see what you're looking for.
- Prices below are the dine-in prices from the restaurant's printed menu. The online ordering menu charges more for the same dishes.

### Allergy Notice

The restaurant's printed notice reads: "Please inform us of any allergies. While we take care in preparing our dishes, we cannot guarantee the absence of allergens due to the variety of ingredients used."
